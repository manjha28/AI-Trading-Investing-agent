# Literature strategies on Delta India: slow, low-turnover (26 Nov 2026)

*Hypothetical backtests on completed bars; kept apart from real P&L. Decision support, not investment advice. **Nothing was traded, armed, deployed or changed.***

## Short answer

- **Nothing clears the bar.** 603 variants in this part (running total about 1703 with equity still to come). The bar: chosen on validation, positive on the holdout, bootstrap 90% interval above zero, and a deflated Sharpe that survives the running total. No strategy meets all four.
- **Closest to a yes: slow trend following with a trailing stop on BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP.** Chosen on validation, the 4h Donchian 50/25 + 3 x ATR trail, long-short, was positive in train, validation and holdout (+525 bps per trade on the holdout, interval +119 to +953) and it is one of a cluster that behaves the same way. But it has about 15 trades per period and a deflated Sharpe of 0.05 after the running total. Of the 53 variants positive on validation and holdout, 20 have a holdout interval above zero; with ~600 variants a pure-noise set would show about 15 such rows, so the count alone proves nothing, but the cluster being the same idea in train too does count for something.
- **Weekly short-the-highest-funding basket:** technically passes (holdout interval +84 to +3,109, 16 baskets) but was -774 in train and its gains come from a handful of small-coin price moves; funding itself is about 280 of the 1,635 bps. I would not trust it.
- Everything else (cross-sectional momentum and reversal, funding contrarian, squeeze, ATR expansion, NR7/inside bar, pairs, hour/weekday, R3 exit variants) fails: negative on validation or holdout, or intervals that include zero.
- The frozen R3 does not repeat its earlier holdout on Delta data with 2 bps slippage (-5.6 bps per trade on the holdout).

## Method, data and what to distrust

- **Sources.** I could not open the papers (I have no page-fetching tool without spawning a sub-agent, which you asked me not to do), so none of the named papers' own rules were read. The definitions are the standard textbook forms of the ideas those papers use: Donchian breakouts, SMA crossovers, EMA(100) trend, time-series momentum with volatility targeting, cross-sectional return ranks as in Liu-Tsyvinski-Wu, funding carry, and pairs by Engle-Granger cointegration. Where a paper's own rule differs, my result may too. The README of statarb-crypto was not read either. Other ideas used: Bollinger squeeze, NR7 and inside bar (Crabel), ATR-expansion breakouts.
- **Data:** Delta India candles and exact funding only. Daily/4h bars built from Delta 1-minute (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and 6 more, from Dec 2023 to Apr 2024 onward) and Delta 15-minute files (183 coins from Nov 2024). 187 crypto perps; the number with history grows from 3-5 (2024) to 187 (Nov 2026), so most coins, and all of the cross-section, exist only in 2025-2026. Today's listing is the universe, so delisted coins are missing (survivorship) and the young coins are today's winners by turnover.
- **Costs:** taker 0.05% + 18% GST, plus 2 bps slippage, on every change of position (7.9 bps per side); exact funding paid and received at 00/08/16 UTC; no maker fills were used in this part. Position weights are sized at entry and held; daily bars use the close as the fill price.
- **Split:** 50/25/25 of the calendar span. Trend, squeeze, ATR, NR7 and R3: 10 Apr 2024 to 24 Nov 2026 (train to about 24 Jul 2025, validation to about 19 Mar 2026, holdout after). Cross-sectional, funding and pairs: 29 Dec 2024 to 24 Nov 2026 (train to 12 Dec 2025, validation to 4 Jun 2026). **These years were used in all earlier tests today, so the holdout is not untouched.** Best variants are chosen on validation Sharpe (R3: validation net bps) with at least 8 validation trades; the tables also show the holdout of every variant for information.
- **'Trade' =** one coin's continuous same-side position (an episode). Net bps are on the notional held, after costs and funding. Max drawdown and Sharpe come from the portfolio's daily net returns (cumulative return, not compounded). Vol targets 10% and 20% scale position size only, so their per-trade bps and Sharpe are identical; both rows are kept and counted.
- **Trend universes:** U4 = BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP; ALL = every coin with 200 days of its own history at that time. 'LO+BTC regime' is long-only and flat while BTC is below its 100-day EMA. Liquidity floor (cross-sectional and funding tests): 30-day mean turnover of USD 2M per day; that leaves 6 to 18 coins, so top/bottom 10 shrinks to the number available.
- **Deflated Sharpe:** N = 1703 trials; variance of daily Sharpe across this part's variants = 0.00293. Bootstrap 90% intervals are for the mean net bps per trade on the holdout.
