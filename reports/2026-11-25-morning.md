### The 25x finding (morning step 6 and this brief's 1.4)
- **Did the gate run?** Yes. Both audit trails show `allowed: true` with `leverage_cap`, `notional_cap`, `risk_per_trade` and `liquidation_safety` all passed.
- **Did they satisfy your rule |entry - stop| <= 0.5 x |entry - liq| with the stop before liquidation?** Yes, by a wide margin: order 1 stop was 268 away vs liquidation 3,307 away (8%); order 2 stop 175 away vs liquidation 3,902 away (4.5%).
- **The rule that was NOT enforced is the 5x effective leverage cap.** It exists only in the scanner's paper sizing. The live gate checks the exchange leverage SETTING (25x) not the leverage actually carried: 1 contract is about 59-60 USD on a wallet of about 3.5-4.1 USD, i.e. **about 14x to 17x effective**. The gate also never read the real balance (the order was sent without knowing whether margin plus fees and GST were covered), and `realized_pnl_today` is hard-coded to 0 so the daily-loss cap could never trip.
- Liquidation in the gate was computed as an isolated 25x position; the account is cross margin, where the whole wallet backs the position.

### The fix (written and tested, NOT deployed)
`backend/app/delta_execution/guardrails.py`, `service.py`, `policy.py`, tests in `tests/test_delta_execution.py` (25 pass, lint clean):
1. Reads the available and total wallet balance from Delta immediately before each order (`read_balances`); unreadable means the order is refused (`balance_known`).
2. `margin_covered`: margin at the set leverage + round-trip taker fees + 18% GST must be within the available balance.
3. `effective_leverage_cap`: order value must be at most 5x the wallet.
4. `liquidation_safety_cross`: under cross margin, liquidation distance is (wallet - maintenance margin) / quantity; the stop must be within half of it.
